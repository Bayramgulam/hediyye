# Demo asset provenance

All product photography in `public/images` was created specifically for this project with the built-in image-generation tool. It is illustrative demo content, not evidence that a merchant sells the depicted goods. No shop images are hotlinked. Replace the catalog imagery, builder cutouts, and configurable hero through authorized admin media controls before launch.

Images were saved locally, cropped/encoded to WebP for use by the application. Alpha from the generated cutout sheet is preserved. Originals remain outside the repository in the generation tool's output directory.

| Files | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| `hero.webp` | Homepage open gift-box hero and packaging story |
| `product-0.webp` … `product-5.webp` | Ceramic mug, candle, chocolate, tea, flowers, closed gift box |
| `gift-0.webp` … `gift-2.webp` | Ivory, blush and olive curated-gift compositions |
| `cutout-0.webp` … `cutout-5.webp` | Transparent top-down builder assets |

## Generation prompts

### Hero

Photorealistic editorial ecommerce hero, landscape 3:2. Open warm ivory rigid gift box with burgundy satin ribbon draped across the foreground, tissue paper, cream ceramic cup, small amber glass candle, artisan chocolate in cream paper, tea tin, blank greeting card and dried floral sprig. Elevated three-quarter perspective, entire box with room at edges, lid behind it. Warm ivory tabletop, pale beige background, soft natural side daylight, tactile paper and satin, realistic fine shadows. Quiet boutique art direction, muted warm grading. No text, watermarks or graphics. Illustrative demo photography for LUMA Azerbaijan.

### Product contact sheet

Coherent contact sheet, exact three columns by two equal square rows with no borders. Identical warm ivory backgrounds, soft daylight, centered top-down products with padding: cream ceramic mug; amber candle with plain cream label; artisan dark chocolate partly unwrapped; ivory tea tin with leaves; dried cream/dusty-rose bouquet; closed ivory gift box with burgundy satin ribbon. No text/watermark; realistic tactile boutique photography. Crop into six individual images.

### Curated gift contact sheet

Three equal square photographic columns, no dividers or text. Soft daylight and warm neutral studio backgrounds. Left: open ivory box with cream mug, wrapped chocolate and tea tin, burgundy ribbon. Center: open dusty-blush box with cream mug, amber candle and dried flowers, rose ribbon. Right: open olive box with chocolate, tea and dried flowers, olive ribbon. Elevated three-quarter views, entire boxes with generous margins, tactile paper/satin and subtle shadows. No letters or watermark; illustrative catalog assets.

### Builder cutouts

Transparent PNG contact sheet, three columns by two equal rows; genuine alpha with no background tiles. Cream mug, amber candle, partly unwrapped artisan chocolate, ivory tea tin, dried floral bouquet and closed ivory gift box with burgundy ribbon. Directly above, softly lit photorealistic objects, shadows confined to objects, each inside its own cell with padding. No text. Intended for isolated product placement in the gift preview.

## Fonts and icons

Self-hosted Noto Serif (regular/italic) and Noto Sans from Fontsource, with Latin and Latin Extended subsets, for Azerbaijani glyph coverage. Noto uses SIL Open Font License 1.1; package license files are included with installed dependencies. Lucide icons use ISC licensing. Decorative wordmark uses the same serif family. The preview is 2D CSS and Motion, with reduced-motion support.
